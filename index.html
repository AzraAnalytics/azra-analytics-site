Selected work

Projects

The firm develops data and AI systems addressing genuine operational challenges across data engineering, advanced analytics, machine learning, and decision-support application development.

Expense Anomaly Detection

Azra Analytics developed an anomaly-detection framework for five years of expense transactions from a major leasing organization. The system combines rule-based controls with Isolation Forest, Histogram-Based Outlier Scores, and denoising autoencoder models to identify duplicate patterns, suspicious amounts, structural rarity, and unusual combinations of transaction features.

Integrated Data and Safety Platform for Transportation

Azra Analytics designed a technical architecture for a trucking and logistics company that integrates operational data from multiple Google Sheets into a centralized data warehouse, automated ETL pipeline, REST API, and dashboard-ready analytics layer, plus predictive modeling from millions of federal inspection records.

Data Ingestion Pipeline for PDFs and Document Images

Azra Analytics designed and prototyped an on-premises AI platform that converts digital and scanned bank statements into validated transaction data and automated financial reports, using document classification, OCR, large language models, and human exception review.
