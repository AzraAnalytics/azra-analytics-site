Fifteen questions on how your organization actually runs today — not on which technology you should buy. You get a scored profile across five dimensions and the three moves that would do the most good next.

Before you start

Most AI projects do not fail on the model. They fail because the data could not be trusted, nobody owned the output, or no one had agreed in advance to act on it. This assessment looks at those conditions.

  • 15 questions, about five minutes.
  • Scored across data foundations, governance and trust, capability and ownership, use-case pipeline, and decision culture.
  • Answer for the part of the organization you are responsible for, and answer honestly — a flattering score is worth nothing to you.
  • Pick the option closest to the truth today, not the one you are working toward.

Your answers stay in your browser. Nothing is sent to Azra Analytics or anyone else unless you choose to email your results at the end.
